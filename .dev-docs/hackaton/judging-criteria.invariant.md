Judging criteria
Judges score each project in 4 categories. 100% in total.

1. Use of Open Mercato and the Agent Orchestrator - 30%
How well did the team use the platform and its AI capabilities?

Is the solution built on Open Mercato?
Does it use the built-in AI features: agents, skills, subagents, tools, workflows?
Do integrations with external services complement the platform rather than replace it?
2. Business value and real-world applicability - 30%
Does it solve a real problem?

Does the agentic solution address a real business need?
Is there a realistic chance of a positive return on investment (ROI)?
Is it clear who would use it and how much time or money it saves?
3. Innovation and creativity - 20%
How creatively did you approach the problem?

Is the idea clever and unconventional?
Does the way you used Open Mercato and its resources surprise us?
Did the team show something nobody expected to see?
4. Completeness of the solution - 20%
Do the agents really close the loop?

Does the agentic process cover the problem end to end - from trigger to final outcome?
How much human involvement does the process require? The less, the higher the score.
Do the key paths work live, not just on slides?
